# Trading Buddy — Simple Daily Guide

Hi! This is your simple guide. You only need to do **one small thing each morning**.
Everything else happens by itself, and the updates come to your phone.

---

## Every morning (before 9:15 AM)

1. Turn on the computer.
2. Find the icon named **Start Trading Buddy** (on the Desktop) and **double-click it**.
3. A black window opens and logs in by itself, then says it's running. **Leave that window open.**

That's the whole morning — **one double-click.** You're done; go about your day.

> The **Daily Login** icon is only a backup — you normally won't need it, because
> Trading Buddy now logs in automatically. Use it only if the black window ever says
> it couldn't log in.

---

## During the day

You don't need to watch the computer. **Just keep your phone nearby.**
Trading Buddy will message you on **Telegram** only when something matters:

| Phone message | What it means |
|---|---|
| 🤖 *Trading Buddy started* | It's running. Good. |
| 📊 *Analysis* + 🟢/🔴 *order* | It found a trade idea and (practice) bought it. |
| 🎯 *EXIT (target)* | A trade closed in **profit**. |
| 🛑 *EXIT (stop)* | A trade closed at a small planned **loss**. |
| 🛑 *KILL SWITCH* | It stopped for the day to protect the money. Nothing to do. |
| 🔔 *EOD Summary* (after 3:30) | The day's recap. |

**Quiet days are normal** — some days there are no trades at all. That's okay and expected.

---

## After 3:30 PM

Nothing to do. You'll get the **🔔 EOD Summary** on your phone.
You can now **close the black window** (click the X) and turn off the computer if you like.

---

## Good to know

- 💚 **This is practice mode (no real money).** Nothing real is being bought or sold.
- 🔌 **Keep the computer on and awake** from 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM (don't let it sleep).
- ❓ If the black window shows an error or closes by itself, don't worry — just tell
  the person who set this up for you. Nothing is broken on your end.

That's the whole guide. One double-click in the morning, and watch your phone. 😊
